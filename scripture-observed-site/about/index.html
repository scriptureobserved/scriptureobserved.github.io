About the project

Faith is part of the story.
So is careful observation.

Why Scripture Observed exists

Scripture Observed is an independent research and documentary project exploring experiences described in and around the Bible through science, research, language, and history.

It is designed for a broad audience. The project does not assume that every reader begins with the same beliefs, experiences, or conclusions. Its job is to make the evidence understandable, show where interpretations differ, and be candid about what remains unknown.

A sister project to OSB Bible

Scripture Observed extends the curiosity behind the OSB Bible YouTube channel into source-led, long-form research that readers can revisit and verify.

Editorial commitments

  • Primary sources and peer-reviewed research are preferred.
  • Fact, interpretation, testimony, and hypothesis are labeled separately.
  • Limitations and uncertainty are included, not hidden.
  • Health information is handled cautiously and is not medical advice.
  • Corrections can be made transparently as the project grows.
