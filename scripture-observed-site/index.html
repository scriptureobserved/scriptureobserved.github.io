Our approach

Curious.
Careful. Clear.

This project is built for people who want to look closely—whether they come from faith, scholarship, personal experience, or simple curiosity.

Trace the source

Claims will link back to original studies, primary texts, or clearly identified expert sources whenever possible.

Separate fact from interpretation

Observed findings, researcher conclusions, and theological interpretations will be labeled distinctly.

Make room for uncertainty

When evidence is limited, mixed, or disputed, we will say so plainly.
